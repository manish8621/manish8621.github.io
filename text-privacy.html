Text — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

In short: Text collects no data. It has no internet access, no ads, no analytics and no accounts. Your files never leave your device.

This policy describes how Text (“the app”), a text editor for Android published by mk playground, handles your information.

Data we collect

None. The app does not request internet permission and contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting software. No personal or usage data is sent to us or to anyone else.

Files you open

Files you open or create are read and written only on your device, through Android’s system file picker or the app that shared the file with Text. The app can only access files you choose.

Data stored on your device

  • Your settings (font size, word wrap, line numbers, auto-save).
  • A list of recently opened files.
  • A temporary copy of unsaved edits, so they are not lost if the app is closed. It is deleted once the file is saved or the edits are discarded.

This data stays in the app’s private storage. Only your settings are included in Android’s device backup. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all of it.

Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. When you choose to share a file’s text with another app, Android hands it directly to the app you pick.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
